Make room for the everyday outdoors

Three simple ways to turn an ordinary walk into something worth remembering.

01 / Notice

Look a little closer

Pick one thing to pay attention to: the shape of a leaf, a bird’s song, or the way light moves across a wall. Familiar places have plenty left to show you.

02 / Wander

Take the scenic way

Try a different street or pause at a nearby park. Keep your route comfortable and safe, and give yourself permission to move at your own pace.

03 / Remember

Bring a moment home

Write down a sentence or sketch something you noticed. Leave plants and wildlife where you found them; a memory is the best thing to collect.

Keep it simple

A good walk doesn’t need much. Here’s a starting point:

  • Comfortable shoes and weather-appropriate layers
  • A bottle of water for the journey
  • A small notebook and a curious mind
